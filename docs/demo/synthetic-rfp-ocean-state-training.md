### 1. Purpose

The Ocean State Regional Workforce Board (the “Board”) seeks a qualified
training provider to design and deliver workforce training programs for adult
learners in information technology and healthcare support occupations across
two counties.

### 2. Scope of work

The selected offeror shall:

1. Design curricula aligned to entry-level IT support and healthcare support
   occupations.
2. Deliver at least four training cohorts during the base year (minimum 20
   learners per cohort).
3. Provide case management and job-placement support for 90 days after cohort
   completion.
4. Report enrollment, completion, and placement metrics monthly.
