# Founding-Customer Offer

## The offer (first 5 Rhode Island firms)

- **$199 flat** — 48-Hour RFP Readiness Package (list $349) on any live RI or
  MA solicitation
- **Guarantee:** if the compliance matrix misses a mandatory requirement,
  it's free
- **Founding-customer terms:** priority turnaround, a direct line to the
  founder, and price locked at $199 for their next two packages
- **The ask in return:** a 15-minute feedback call after delivery, and — only
  if they're genuinely happy — permission to use a short quote

Scarcity is real, not theatrical: concierge-level attention on the first five
deliveries is how we harden quality, and the XPRIZE timeline caps how many we
can hand-hold.

## The value math (what you say in the room)

- A typical small-firm RFP response consumes **15–30 staff hours**. At even a
  $75/hr blended rate that's **$1,125–$2,250 of labor** — before the owner's
  opportunity cost.
- Most of those hours go to *decoding the RFP*, not selling: finding every
  mandatory requirement, forms, insurance floors, formatting rules.
- BidPilot compresses that to a **$199 package delivered in 48 hours**:
  compliance matrix with page citations, bid/no-bid recommendation, and a
  first draft grounded in the firm's own past performance and materials.
- The failure mode it prevents is total loss: a **non-responsive bid scores
  zero** regardless of quality. One missed form erases the entire 20-hour
  investment.
- For firms that decide *not* to bid, $199 to confidently skip a bad-fit
  RFP saves the full 20 hours — the no-bid outcome is a win too.

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already have someone who writes proposals" | "Great — this makes them faster. The matrix and draft land in their inbox 48 hours after you send the RFP; they polish instead of decode." |
| "Can AI really do this?" | "The AI drafts; every requirement is cited to a page of the RFP so a human verifies in minutes, and you review everything before anything is submitted. Plus the guarantee: miss a mandatory requirement, it's free." |
| "$199 seems too cheap to be good" | "Founding price — it's $349 after the first five RI firms. We're buying feedback and testimonials, and we're honest about it." |
| "We don't do government work" | "That's exactly who RI wants bidding — the state has participation goals for local and MBE/WBE vendors. First package is the cheapest way to test the market you've ever seen." |
| "Send me info" | Send sample + one specific live opportunity that fits them, and book the follow-up before hanging up. |

## What "sold" requires (XPRIZE evidence discipline)

Every founding customer must be **arms-length**: no friends, family, or
anyone with a pre-existing financial relationship. Payment through the live
Stripe checkout (never manual invoicing, never mock mode), a real
solicitation, real delivery, and the transaction recorded with the customer's
org in the revenue ledger.
