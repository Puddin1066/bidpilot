# Vendor Mapping: 5–10 Plausible Vendors per Opportunity

Budget 20–30 minutes per opportunity. The goal is *plausible bidders* — firms
that do this exact work at this scale in this geography — not a broad list.

## Where to find them

Work these in order; stop when you have 5–10 solid names.

1. **Past award history for the same buyer.** OSP's "View Award Reports" shows
   bid opening reports and final awards on prior solicitations — the losing
   bidders from last cycle are the warmest possible list. RIVIP and COMMBUYS
   also expose prior awards. A firm that bid and lost last time is the ideal
   prospect: they want to win, and they already believe in the market.
2. **Google Maps / local search** for the service category + city
   (e.g., "HVAC contractor Warwick RI", "IT managed services Providence").
   Firms with 5–50 employees and a real service page for the scoped work.
3. **LinkedIn company search** filtered by region and industry; check they
   actually list the relevant service.
4. **Trade association directories** (RI Builders Association, Tech Collective,
   RI Manufacturers Association, chamber directories).
5. **State vendor / MBE-WBE directories.** RI's certified MBE/WBE list is
   public and many solicitations carry participation goals — being on the
   list signals active interest in government work.

## Qualifying a vendor (all four must be plausible)

- **Capability:** their website shows they deliver the scoped service.
- **Capacity:** 3–50 employees. Solo operators can't staff it; 100+ firms have
  proposal staff and won't pay us.
- **Geography:** RI or within ~90 min for site-visit-type work; wider for
  remote-deliverable services.
- **Reachability:** you can find a named principal (owner, president, BD lead)
  and a direct or guessable email. Info@ is a last resort.

## Finding the contact

1. Website "About"/"Team" page → owner or principal's name.
2. LinkedIn → confirm title, sometimes email in contact info.
3. Email pattern: try first@domain, first.last@domain — verify with a free
   checker before sending; if unverifiable, use the site contact form and log
   it as a touch.
4. Phone number from the website — a call is a valid touch and often better
   than email for trades.

## Log every vendor

One row per contact in `tracker/prospects.csv`, linked to the opportunity by
solicitation number. Fill the personalization fields (`hook_requirement`,
`personal_note`) **at mapping time** while context is fresh — this makes
send-day a 2-minute-per-email job instead of a research session.
