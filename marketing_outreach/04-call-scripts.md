# Call Scripts

Two referral-partner calls (Day 3 of the launch plan) plus the warm-prospect
script. Referral partners matter more than any single prospect: APEX and SBDC
each talk to dozens of RI firms per week who are *exactly* our customer.

---

## Call 1 — RI APEX Accelerator

- **Who:** Rhode Island APEX Accelerator (partner program of RI Commerce),
  315 Iron Horse Way, Suite 101, Providence. Main line **401-278-9100**;
  site riapex.org.
- **Named contacts** (verify current roles on riapex.org/contact before
  calling): Chad Lewis, State Director (401-278-9145); Jake Bacon
  (401-278-9133); Ann Garcia (401-278-9152); Jeff Brant (401-278-9125).
- **What they do:** free counseling for RI firms on government contracting —
  registrations, bid match, solicitation analysis, proposal review.
- **Positioning:** we are *complementary*, not competitive. They advise;
  clients still have to produce the compliance matrix and draft themselves.
  BidPilot is the tool their clients use to do the work between counseling
  sessions.

**Script:**

> Hi, this is {name} — I'm a Rhode Island founder and I've built a tool
> called BidPilot that helps small RI firms respond to state and municipal
> RFPs. It reads a solicitation and produces a compliance matrix, a
> bid/no-bid analysis, and a grounded first draft in 48 hours, flat fee
> under $350.
>
> I'm not calling to sell you anything — your services are free and ours
> aren't, and I know the difference. I'm calling because you see firms every
> week who decide not to bid because they can't spare 20 hours for a
> proposal. Two asks:
>
> 1. Could I show one of your advisors a sample report — 15 minutes — so you
>    know exactly what it is before you'd ever mention it to a client?
> 2. What would BidPilot have to prove for you to feel comfortable
>    mentioning it as one option when a client says they don't have time to
>    respond?

**If asked "is it AI?":** "Yes — AI-assisted, with every requirement cited to
the page of the RFP so a human can verify it in minutes. Nothing goes out
without the client reviewing it, and if we miss a mandatory requirement the
package is free."

**Leave-behind:** follow up same day with the sample-report link and a
3-sentence description they can forward.

## Call 2 — RI Small Business Development Center (RISBDC)

- **Who:** RISBDC at the University of Rhode Island. Lead office (Kingston)
  **401-874-7232** (401-874-SBDC), weekdays 8:30–4:00. Providence Metro
  office is at 315 Iron Horse Way (same building as APEX). Site:
  web.uri.edu/risbdc.
- **What they do:** free counseling on business planning, finance, marketing.
  Less procurement-specific than APEX — so pitch on the *revenue growth*
  angle, not procurement mechanics.

**Script:**

> Hi, this is {name}, a Rhode Island founder. I've built BidPilot — it helps
> small RI businesses win state and municipal contracts by handling the RFP
> paperwork: compliance checklist, bid/no-bid, and a first draft in 48
> hours for a flat fee.
>
> A lot of your clients could be selling to the state — it's steady revenue
> and RI actively wants local and minority- and women-owned vendors — but
> they never bid because the paperwork is brutal. Two asks:
>
> 1. Which counselor handles clients interested in government sales or
>    revenue diversification? I'd love to send them a sample report.
> 2. Do you have upcoming workshops or newsletters where "how to sell to
>    the state" would be a fit? I'll present or write it for free, no pitch.

**Note:** SBDC will likely refer procurement questions to APEX — that's fine;
it validates the APEX relationship. Take the referral graciously and ask ask
#2 anyway (workshops/newsletter reach a different audience).

## Warm-prospect call script (replied to email, or trades where email is weak)

> Hi {first_name}, this is {name} — I emailed you about the {buyer}
> {short_title} RFP that closes {deadline}. Got two minutes?
>
> [If yes] The reason I flagged it: {hook_requirement}. Firms get bounced on
> that kind of thing all the time and never find out why they lost.
>
> Here's the offer: for $199 flat, within 48 hours you get every mandatory
> requirement in a checklist with page citations, an honest bid/no-bid
> recommendation, and a first draft grounded in your own materials. If we
> miss a mandatory requirement, you pay nothing.
>
> Were you planning to bid this one?

- **"Yes, bidding":** "Then the readiness package is cheap insurance — want
  me to send the checkout link while we're on the phone?"
- **"Undecided":** "That's literally what the bid/no-bid analysis is for —
  $199 to make a 20-hour decision with real information."
- **"No":** "Fair. Want me to flag the next one that fits you? Free to be on
  that list." → log as NURTURE.

## After every call

Log in `tracker/prospects.csv`: date, outcome (NO_ANSWER / VOICEMAIL /
CONVERSATION / REFERRAL / MEETING_BOOKED), and next action with a date.
Voicemail counts as a touch — leave the 15-second version: name, their RFP,
one requirement, callback number.
